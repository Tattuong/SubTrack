## Full description

SubTrack keeps the price, cycle, and next payment date for the services you type in. See a monthly and yearly equivalent, a renewal timeline, and a reminder you set yourself.

Save the manage link and a note about how to cancel. Mark a plan active, paused, or cancelled. SubTrack only updates its own list. It does not read a bank account or cancel the service.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and SubTrack Pro (more than 8 plans) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and SubTrack Pro are one-time unlocks.

Privacy Policy – SubTrack

SubTrack is a private, on-device reminder for renewals you type in. Plan names, prices, dates, links, cancel notes, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account, read a bank, or cancel a service.

Data stored on the device

  • Plans, renewal dates, prices, links, and notes
  • Coin balance and shop unlocks
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and SubTrack Pro

Services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and SubTrack Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N311.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
